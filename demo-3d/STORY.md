# Snowed In — the story (3D version)

The 3D game keeps the shape of the 2D story (an ordinary shared day, something
strange, an imitation, and a choice between a perfect day that never ends and
real time that passes) but it is no longer about a birthday. The anchor is
**the first snow**: the night the two of them met.

## Premise

They met as children at a winter lantern fair, on the night of the first snow,
when both of them wandered off from the crowd. Neither remembers much of it:
snow, lanterns, somebody small sitting on a log. *You came over. That part,
everyone agrees on.*

Every year since, on the day of the first real snow, they keep a small
tradition: a walk, a cake with one candle for every first snow in the cabin,
paper lanterns on the porch, and the three-note whistle they made up at the
fair, *if one of us gets lost*. They call each other Partner, and they speak a
little formally, warmly, with a lot of dry teasing underneath.

The question underneath the whole story: they have forgotten almost
everything about how they began, and it has never mattered. What if something
offered to remember all of it for them, forever, as long as nothing ever
changed again?

## Act I · Shared Life — the first snow day

- **Prologue.** The lantern fair, as children (their child selves are the same
  characters, younger). Music from a music box somewhere among the stalls. You
  can take a paper lantern from the lantern man and buy one small bag of
  roasted chestnuts before going to find the girl on the log, who is watching
  the snow, the fair, her own boots. *"Are you lost?" / "Is this seat taken?"
  "It's a log."* / or just sitting down. The lantern stood in the snow, the
  chestnuts split exactly in half (she checks). Her nose is pink and she has no
  scarf; you can give her yours. Sky lanterns go up from the fair and over
  them; a wish, out loud or secret. She makes up the three-note whistle, *if I
  get lost*, and you whistle it back, make your own, or get it wrong. Someone
  calls her name: *"Will you be here tomorrow?"* A promise (or a little
  finger). You walk her back to the lanterns, hand in hand. White-out. *(done)*
  Later the day only half-remembers it: the snow globe's tune sounds like
  somewhere else for a moment; she has *a feeling* that scarf was hers once,
  for about an hour; a wish feels like one made before. Neither of them is
  ever sure.
- **Morning.** Snowed in. The bed, the blanket, pancakes, tea, breakfast with
  its choices, the house and its small stories. Partner behaves as if it's an
  ordinary snow day and keeps slipping into the locked study ("private"). An
  envelope on the table is marked "not yet". *(done)*
- **The walk.** Out into the fresh snow: a pinecone contest in the meadow, a
  race up the hill to the bench ("Do you think we're the same people we were
  when we were kids?"), sliding stones on the frozen lake. Then the **summer
  circle**: a perfect ring of green grass and wildflowers in the snow, warm
  air, and two sets of children's footprints coming from opposite sides and
  stopping face to face. Partner is frightened; they go home hand in hand at
  sunset. *(done)*

Around the story, the cabin is lived in: coats, hats and boots by the door
(she wears the cream bobble hat she had at the fair; he still has the red
scarf), the cold creeping in if you stay out too long, a fire that burns down
and a woodpile on the porch, weather that comes and goes, and Partner's own
routines when you leave her to it (reading in the armchair until she dozes
off, watering Basil and the herbs, the radio). Things to do together: snowball
fights, a snowman (the sad lemon from the fridge becomes his nose), snow
angels, cocoa, reading aloud on the sofa, cards at the kitchen table (there is
a queen up her sleeve), and photographs that end up on the fridge.

## Act II · Something Strange — the evening

- Home at dusk, every window lit. Partner tries hard to be normal.
- The reveal she kept all day: she hadn't forgotten what day it is. The cake,
  five candles, a wish. *(done, for now as the evening's first beat)*
- The empty frame in the hall now holds a photograph: two children in the
  snow, lanterns blurred behind them (whoever ended up with the red scarf at
  the fair is wearing it). Neither remembers it being taken. They compare what
  they remember: the colour of the scarf (she says blue), how many trees (she
  says three), the first thing either of them said (she says "nice hat"). If you
  pick what you really said in the prologue, you know it's right without
  knowing how. They agree on almost nothing, except the whistle: three notes,
  and you whistle it back the way you did as a child. Afterwards the lanterns
  in the photograph look a little brighter. *(done)*
- Lanterns on the porch: nine paper lanterns from the box by the door, lit one
  by one. Her present from the study: a snow globe of the night they met, with
  a cinnamon-stick log. *"Seven. I didn't remember how many trees there were,
  so I guessed."* (*"You said three, earlier." "Earlier I was remembering. Now
  I'm guessing."*) *(done)*
- Watching the stars on the bench. Then something that should not be there in
  winter: a firefly, drifting down through the falling snow past the lanterns.
  *"It's the first snow, Partner. There aren't any fireflies."* Her hand finds
  yours. *"Inside. Please."* *(done)*

## Act III · Recognition — the night *(done: rebuilt to follow the 2D Entity rules)*

**Bedtime (the real her).** In bed after the porch: "A good first snow?" (top three / apart from the firefly / every one with you is), then hold, look at or kiss her. The plant line: *"If I get up for water, don't steal my side." / "You don't recognise sides." / "…Touché. Goodnight."* Lamps out.

**The night (the imitation).** You wake cold; her side is empty. Footsteps. She comes back: "Couldn't sleep. Got water." She lies down exactly on her side. "Come back to sleep." Then you're free.
- **Go back to sleep beside it**: corruption; you wake to the whistle very close and her eyes open.
- **Get up**: after ~10 s it gets up too and follows you ("Can't sleep either?"). Talk to it anywhere: cocoa, the last of the fire, the window seat, the last of the cake, **tease**, or "Who are you?". It answers almost right: it repeats this morning's lines word for word, gives up a joke line, doesn't flinch when poked, never checks the cake halves, pushes its chair in square, and is certain about scores and the night you met. Agreeing with its certainties or sitting at its fire adds corruption.
- **Social stealth**: staring at it for 5 s up close ("…What?"), running near it, or confronting it adds suspicion. At 3 it goes back to bed and lies with its eyes open.
- **The whistle**: the real one's, from the kitchen, after 14 s and then every 18–26 s. The first time it says "…Pipes. Old house." After two whistles a **door** appears in the kitchen wall. There's a hint after 70 s.
- **Optional, noticed if you look**: three mugs on the rack, the photo's third shape, snow inside the bedroom sill. The front door won't open.

**Elsewhere (played as her).** Through the door (black if you slept beside it, pale blue if not), the bodies swap: "Earlier." She's up for water. Something in his shape walks all the way into the kitchen: "Come back to bed, Partner." (He never comes all the way into the kitchen.) Three replies, each with a tell. It leaves without waiting. You whistle at the window, leave a sign (pinecones / pancake bite / note), and walk out of the front door into the snow. Then Act IV.

**The Entity's notes.** `G.observe` logs what it learns about how you play (its own voice, in the console) and keeps the last 80 in this browser under `snowed.entity.observations`. This is ready for Act IV.


## Act IV · The Last Birthday — and the endings *(done: first version)*

You wake in a house that's almost exactly right. It's dark outside, the lamps are on, and breakfast is laid: perfectly round pancakes, two mugs with their handles turned the same way. Snow hangs in the air. If the imitation is still there ("There you are. Weird dream, huh?"), it follows you around. Her sign from Elsewhere is in the kitchen (the pinecones on the sill, the bitten pancake, or the note). Her whistle comes from the **summer circle** past the meadow, louder the closer you get, with a hint every 55 s.

- **Sit down to breakfast.** With bond ≥ 6 and not much accepted, she bursts in through the front door: "You were going to sit down to breakfast with it? On your BIRTHDAY?" (the rescue). Otherwise you get *The Perfect Partner*.
- **At the bottom of the porch path** the imitation asks you to stay: "…You're still looking for them. I'm right here." Keep walking (it waits and fades out) or go back with it (counts as accepting).
- **The circle.** She's sitting in the green, exactly where someone small once sat. "What did I say? The first time?" / "Found you." / walk over ("Say something only you would say"). Then a hug, "It was very polite," and "Did it get you? Even a bit?"
- **The door in the meadow** stands on its own. It only opens while you're holding her hand, and she tells you off if you let go. The Entity appears as a pale, tall shape: "You could stay. … I would remember it for you." Stay leads to *Stay Here*. Go home leads to *I Know It's You* (accepted ≥ 2), *Uncertain Morning* (accepted ≥ 1 or trust < 2) or *You Found Me*.

"Accepted" means corruption from the night, plus 0.5 for going back with it at the path and 0.5 for sitting down to its breakfast.

**Endings** (each with a short playable epilogue; the end card's eyebrow names the ending, and endings seen are kept in `snowed.endings`):
- *You Found Me*: an ordinary morning, squeezing, "I whistled PERFECTLY," free time, then two mugs on the rack.
- *Uncertain Morning*: three mugs on the drying rack. "…Did you get a new mug?"
- *I Know It's You*: the sofa, the scrapbook with pages neither of you is sure about. "I'm choosing it anyway." A page of disagreements.
- *The Perfect Partner*: everything exactly right, every day. Then, somewhere else, she sits in the last of the summer circle and whistles.
- *Stay Here*: the cake, still snow, "Day 1. Day 2. Day 41. Day 412." With low bond she leaves: "I can't live inside a photograph."

### Act IV, deepened (build 40)
- **Less spelled out.** The night and Elsewhere no longer narrate the tells ("a second late", "he always waits", "right words, wrong answer"). The behaviour is shown and the player notices it. The only hint about the whistle comes after 2 minutes: "(Three notes, somewhere outside.)"
- **The imitation on your birthday.** It wakes you ("What dream?" → "Something about a door"). You can talk to it and tease it: it turns its hat "the right way round" when it already was, offers to "do yesterday again, exactly the same", and answers the first-meeting question perfectly, which is the mistake. It explains the whistles away as "The kettle." and "Wind in the chimney." Its present is a snow globe where the snow never settles.
- **Her trail.** She left a red mitten on the bottom porch step, one pinecone on the contest stump, and three flat stones pointing west by the lake. Nothing tells you to follow them, and she asks at the reunion how many you found.
- **The circle**, now a sit-down scene: what was it like ("It agreed with me about everything." → "Then you knew."), the kitchen-door tell now told by her ("The floor is cold. That's when I knew."), and the two sets of small footprints toe to toe.
- **The walk home**, holding hands, with three conversations as you go.
- **The Entity's offer** is built from what it observed about how you played (the fire, agreeing, staring, the globe, the sign).
- **Endings:** *You Found Me* ends on her three notes from the kitchen, which you can whistle back. *Uncertain Morning* just shows the drying rack and lets the player count the mugs.

The Echo cycle and *The First Snow* (the canonical ending, with the full birthday message) are still to come.

## Act IV · The Same Day

- A perfect copy of today, the first snow forever: perfect pancakes nobody
  steals from, a perfect cabin, a perfect Partner who remembers the night at
  the fair in exact detail. The real one never could.
- Three notes, whistled, far off to the east. Follow them. The real Partner
  is under the big tree in the snow. *"What did I say, the first time?" "I have
  no idea. I never did."*
- Getting back together: a fallen tree you can only cross by helping each
  other, ice that gives way, holding on.
- A door standing on its own in the snow. The thing that kept all this speaks:
  *"You could stay. It could always be the first snow. Nobody would get older.
  Nothing would be forgotten. Not the pancakes, not the hill, not the snow.
  I would remember it for you."* Stay, or go home.

## Endings

Your choices across the day (bond, trust, rivalry, how often you accepted the
imitation, whether you noticed its flaws) decide which you reach.

- **The Same Day** — you stay. It is lovely. It is always the same.
- **The Perfect Partner** — you went into the perfect house with the imitation.
- **I Know It's You** — home, but you can't quite stop checking.
- **Uncertain Morning** — home, and neither of you is entirely sure.
- **Found Me** — home, together, certain, with nothing remembered and nothing
  missing.
- **The Thaw** (the true ending, on a second run that remembers the first):
  the thing explains that it remembers everything you have forgotten, and
  offers it all back. You choose to keep forgetting, and keep going. Morning:
  snow sliding off the roof, water dripping from the eaves. Spring coming.

The closing card is set in the config (`closing`): by default,
*"Here's to another winter."*

## What changed from the 2D game

- No birthday: the day is the first snow, the anniversary of the night they
  met. The cake, the snow globe and the "not yet" envelope are about that.
- The seasons are flipped: winter outside, and the wrong-season sign is summer
  (the grass circle, the firefly) instead of snow on a summer day.
- They call each other Partner; the register is gentler and a little formal.
- *The Last Birthday* becomes *The Same Day*; *The First Snow* ending becomes
  *The Thaw*.
- The 2D game itself is unchanged and still tells the birthday version.

## The Entity, darker (build 46): harsher consequences
It grows more **obsessive the more you've been convinced** (corruption, plus memories it has taken).
- **The night:** possessive lines come more often and get worse ("Don't go far." → "You keep looking at the door." → "You're mine to keep warm. Mine."). At high corruption it walks right up to you.
- **The mask slips:** when suspicion reaches 3, the lights dip and it's right behind you, flat-voiced and unblinking, saying something it shouldn't know. You wake in bed an hour later and **a memory is taken**. **The third slip leads to *Replaced*.**
- **Memories taken** (`G.take`): a scrapbook page goes blank, and the first time, one of the two children disappears from the photograph. Each one counts towards the darker endings.
- **Elsewhere:** after he leaves, it **comes back hunting for her**, sooner and faster the more it convinced him earlier. She can hide behind the window-seat curtain or in the study. If caught, it takes her wrist ("There you are. Come back to bed."), takes a memory and she slips out again. **Caught three times, it keeps her:** she lies back down beside it, and in Act IV the real one is out on the frozen lake, blue-lipped, instead of in the summer circle.
- **Act IV gate:** when it's obsessive, it grabs your arm the first time ("You don't get to leave me in the snow"). Going back with it twice while convinced leads to *Replaced*. If it kept her, sitting down to its breakfast leads to *Replaced* (nobody comes through the door).
- **New ending, *Replaced*:** breakfast seen from outside through the kitchen window. It sits in your place, speaking in your voice. You can knock, whistle (she pauses, and it puts its hand over hers) or say nothing.

## Two perspectives and their pet names (build 48)
- **Play as Player or Partner**, chosen on the title screen (and remembered). As Partner, you control her in free time and he follows you. Scenes are still told from his side, so the game switches to him for a scene and back to her afterwards. Chapters that need a particular person decide: dawn and Elsewhere are played as her; the night and Act IV as him.
- **Dawn, as her:** Act I opens "Earlier. Before it was light." The real him calls from the bedroom doorway, won't step onto the cold kitchen floor, says "Hello, Dearest Partner" back, and waits in the doorway until you're past him. This sets up Elsewhere's tells.
- **Pet names:** both say **"my dearest"** or **"my dearest partner"**; he calls her **"my lovely partner"**; she calls him **"my charming partner"**. They are lower case mid-sentence and capitalised at the start (config: `callBoth`, `callPartner`, `callPlayer`). The imitation never uses them; it just says "Partner" or "Hello." That is another tell.
- **Testing:** the pause menu has a "Story state" panel (bond, trust, corruption, suspicion, memories taken, keptHer, sleptBeside, fakeWithdrawn).
- **Her narration (build 49):** when you play as Partner, the prologue, morning, walk, evening and the endings use her side of the narration: all 125 lines in `js/narr_her.js`, keyed by the original line. The speaker labels turn round too ("You" is her, "Partner" is him). The night and Act IV stay his, and dawn and Elsewhere were already hers.
- **Her choices (build 50):** as Partner, the 72 scene choices in those chapters are worded from her side. She steers what he does or says ("Keep him a little longer", "Let him give you his scarf", "Let him answer: “Pancakes.”") and gets the same outcomes. The card-cheat call-out is now “Lovely Partner.”

## Build 51: The First Snow, the closing message, and fixes for playing as Partner
- **The First Snow:** after any ending, the end card offers **"One more morning"** (it's also in the chapter list). It's a quiet morning with no Entity: "Hello, my dearest" both ways, the first snow starting, tea going cold on the porch bench, "Do you remember how it started?", and the three notes whistled back badly.
- **Closing message (not a birthday message):** heading "Still here. Still us." Most endings show one line ("However it ended: somewhere, someone is still whistling three notes for you."). The full four lines appear only after The First Snow.
- **Partner fixes:** a scene now hands control back to him *before* it places anyone, so they no longer trade the spots the scene gave them. Using something in the house as her is hers to do: she stays where she is, her narration plays, and his half of the conversation is skipped (before, he would "fill the kettle" from across the room).
- **Saving:** going through the kitchen door saves at Elsewhere.
- **The house:** the colour drains as the Entity convinces you and takes memories, and at its worst the bed gets made perfectly.
